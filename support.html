Frequently asked questions

How do I share a round with my group?

Start a round, then tap the sync code in the top bar (or the QR button). Friends tap JOIN ROUND on their home screen and enter the code, or scan the QR with their camera.

Scores aren't syncing between phones.

Both phones need an internet connection and must be in the same round code. Scores entered offline are saved on the device and sync the next time the app is online. If a round looks stuck, exit the round (EXIT tab) and rejoin with the same code — nothing is lost.

How do payouts work?

Every game — the money games (Wolf, Nassau, Skins, Pass the Money, Bingo Bango Bongo, Tee Ball, Markey Match) and the match formats (stroke play, match play, four-ball, scrambles, Stableford, Quota and the rest) — settles from the stake you set during round setup. Set a stake of zero to play a format for pride only. The Results screen adds it all up, shows who owes whom in the fewest transfers, and can open a Venmo request pre-filled with the exact amount for any player who has a handle on their profile. PlayPal never holds or moves money.

How do I add a course that isn't listed?

On the course selection step, choose ADD A COURSE MANUALLY and enter par, yardage, and handicap for each hole. The course is saved to your library and synced to your group.

How do I delete my data?

Player profiles can be deleted in the app (open the profile → DELETE). To erase everything associated with you from the sync database, email jchristadore@gmail.com — see the Privacy Policy.

Does PlayPal handle real money?

No. PlayPal only does the math. Settling up is between you and your group.

Reporting a bug

Groups: who can see my rounds?

PlayPal has no accounts. Your install belongs to a group identified by a long random code, and player profiles, custom courses and shared rounds sync only inside it. Home → Your Group → MANAGE shows your code, lets you paste someone else's to join theirs, or starts a fresh empty group. Sharing a round by QR code or link includes the group, so a playing partner who taps it lands in the right place automatically.

Adding a course that isn't listed

New Round → Course → ADD A COURSE. Take a photo of the scorecard (it stays on your device — nothing is uploaded) and it sits above the entry grid so you can read the small print while you fill it in. If you can copy the numbers from the club's website, paste them into Paste the numbers and the grid fills itself in; it works out which row is par, which is yardage and which is the stroke index.

Posting scores to GHIN

PlayPal can't post to GHIN for you — there is no public API for it. The Results → SEND tab copies every player's hole-by-hole scores along with the tees, rating and slope, formatted to paste straight into GHIN's own score entry.

Email a description of what happened, what you expected, and your device/browser to jchristadore@gmail.com. Screenshots help a lot.
