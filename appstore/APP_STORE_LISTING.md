## Description (4000 chars max)
```
PlayPal is the scorekeeper your golf group has been arguing without.

Pick your players, pick your games, and score the round once — PlayPal keeps every side game straight and settles the math before you reach the parking lot.

EIGHT SIDE GAMES, ONE SCORECARD
• Wolf — rotating wolf, partners or lone-wolf doubles
• Nassau — front, back, and overall, 1v1 or 2v2, with presses
• Skins — carryovers handled automatically
• Stableford — points without the mental math
• Pass the Money — the chip moves, PlayPal watches it
• Bingo Bango Bongo — three points a hole, zero disputes
• Tee Ball — longest drive in play takes the point
• Markey Match — team match play with auto-presses

Plus common stroke and match formats (stroke play, match play, four-ball, scrambles, quota, and more) so one card covers the whole day.

LIVE SYNC FOR THE WHOLE GROUP
Share a short code or QR and every phone in the group sees scores update in real time. One person scores; everyone watches.

PAYOUTS, DONE
Set the stakes during setup and the results screen shows who owes whom — with optional Venmo links to settle up. PlayPal never holds or moves money; it only does the arithmetic.

GOLF TRIPS
Group rounds into trips for a running leaderboard, earnings table, birdie counts, and trip awards.

BUILT FOR THE COURSE
Works offline when the back nine loses signal. Handicaps and pops applied per hole. Custom courses with full scorecard entry. Optional sign-in for PlayPal Pro. No ads and no third-party analytics or trackers.
```

## FAQ (also on support.html)
- **Does it handle money?** No — math only; settle on Venmo or in cash.
- **Do I need an account?** Core scoring works without one. Sign-in is optional (group ownership / PlayPal Pro).
- **Does it work offline?** Yes; scores sync when you're back online.
- **Can I add my home course?** Yes — full 18-hole scorecard entry.
